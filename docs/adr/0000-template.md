# NNNN: Title in the form of the decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, what is measured or known. Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What we do, stated so it can be checked in the code or the infrastructure.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, what this costs, and the trigger that would make us revisit it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option**: why not, in one line.
